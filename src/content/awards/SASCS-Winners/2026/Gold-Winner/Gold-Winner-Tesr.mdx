**3\. Briefly describe the nominated team: history and past performance (up to 1,250 characters)::**

In January 2023, an online provider of legal services faced a critical challenge: their rapidly growing online business needed to hit ambitious revenue targets promised to investors, but internal chat agents were defrauding the system and undermining performance. To drive sustainable growth, the client partnered with VXI to build a high-performing, scalable chat sales operation from the ground up.VXI expanded the team from 20 to 34 chat sales representatives in Davao, Philippines, introducing end-to-end operational rigor including SOPs, process documentation, quality assurance, and performance metrics. Sellers received comprehensive training in chat sales best practices, upselling, objection handling, and sales culture, while leadership participated in coaching, sales strategy workshops, and alignment sessions to cultivate a

results-driven environment. Sales incentives and contests served as key motivators, reinforcing performance and accountability.Leveraging Salesforce Customer Connect, AI-powered chat optimization, and automated post-chat follow-ups, the team delivered measurable results. In just six months of 2025, VXI’s chat sales team generated $5.6M in incremental revenue, achieving $128.57 revenue per chat (vs.

$120 target), a 41.47% conversion rate (vs. 35% target), and an average order value of $439.51 (vs. $245 target). Through the combination of people, process, and technology, the VXI partnership transformed a struggling channel into a best-in-class online sales operation for its legal services client.
