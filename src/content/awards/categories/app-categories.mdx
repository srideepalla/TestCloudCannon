These categories recognize excellence in mobile websites and apps since the beginning of 2024.<br>Nominations that won in the 2025 International Business Awards® may be resubmitted for consideration in the 2026 awards. If they have been updated with recent achievements, they may be submitted to the same categories in which they won. If they have not been updated, they must be submitted to categories different from those in which they won.

**Information to be submitted online for entries in these categories in the 2026 competition include:**

**1\. The mobile site or app itself, in the form of a demonstration video of up to three (3) minutes in length. You must attach the video to your entry through our attachment/link uploading tool.**

**2. Written answers to the following questions, describing the nominated achievements since 1 January 2024:**<br>a. The date on which this nominated app or site was first distributed or otherwise made public. *Required*<br>b. Briefly describe this mobile site or app’s communications objectives and how it met those objectives since the beginning of 2024 (up to 1,500 characters) If applicable, include the platforms your app supports. *Required*<br>c. List the appropriate creative and production credits for this entry (up to 1,500 characters). *Optional*<br>d. Reference any attachments of supporting materials throughout this nomination and how they provide evidence of the claims you have made in this nomination (up to 1,500 characters). *Optional*

**3\. Optional (but highly recommended), add to your entry up to 10 webpage links and/or document uploads to support your case to the judges.These might be press articles, press releases, videos, work samples, internal or external reviews, or employee feedback, for example.**
