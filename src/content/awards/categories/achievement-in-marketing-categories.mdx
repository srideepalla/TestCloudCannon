## **Marketing Organization/Professional Categories**

**Information to be submitted online for entries in these categories in the 2026 competition include:**

**1\. Written answers to the following questions, describing the nominated achievements since 1 January 2024, OR a video of up to five (5) minutes in length that answers all of the same questions:**<br>a. Briefly describe the nominated organization, department, team, or individual: its history and past performance (up to 1,500 characters). *Required*<br>b. Outline the achievements since the beginning of 2024 that you wish to bring to the judges' attention (up to 1,900 characters). *Required*<br>c. Explain why the achievements you have highlighted are unique or significant. If possible, compare the achievements to the performance of other players in your industry and/or to the organization's past performance (up to 1,900 characters). *Required*<br>d. Reference any attachments of supporting materials throughout this nomination and how they provide evidence of the claims you have made in this nomination (up to 1,500 characters). *Optional*

**2\. Optional (but highly recommended), add to your entry up to 10 webpage links and/or document uploads to support your case to the judges.These might be press articles, press releases, videos, work samples, internal or external reviews, or employee feedback, for example.**

G01. **Marketing Department of the Year**<br>Recognizes the recent achievements of everyone who works in marketing functions in your organization, regardless of function, location, seniority, or position.

G02. **Marketing Team of the Year**<br>Recognizes some subset of your entire marketing organization, such as a specific product marketing, brand marketing, social media or creative team. The team may be multidisciplinary, may span multiple organizations (your own organization, client organizations, and agency or vendor organizations, for example), and may be a permanent team or a temporary team that was assembled only for a specific task or assignment.

G03. **Marketing Executive of the Year**<br>Enter this category to recognize the achievements of individual marketing executives at the VP level or above.

G50. **Best Use of AI-Powered Marketing Strategy (New for 2026)**<br>Recognizes holistic marketing strategies that leveraged AI to drive planning, personalization, and optimization across channels.

G51. **Best Use of Data to Drive Brand Strategy**<br>Recognizes campaigns that have effectively developed from the successful use of data and technology to identify and match the right audiences to the right message at the right moments.

G52. **Brand Experience of the Year - Business-to-Business**<br>Recognizes work that reaches out to business audiences to establish meaningful relationships, memorable, engaging experiences, and unique connections with their brands.

G53. **Brand Experience of the Year - Consumer**<br>Recognizes work that reaches out to consumer audiences to establish meaningful relationships, memorable, engaging experiences, and unique connections with their brands.

G54. **Branded Content Campaign of the Year**<br>Recognizes work that has used branded content—original or sponsored—to reach audiences and establish meaningful relationships, memorable, engaging experiences, and unique connections with their brands.

G55. **Branded Utility of the Year**<br>Recognizes products and services created to address a marketing or business challenge, not to be sold, as part of a marketing program itself. Entrants must explain the product or service created in response to the challenge, its roles and objectives, how it was communicated, and results achieved.

G56. **Customer Loyalty & Retention Campaign of the Year (New for 2026)**<br>Recognizes marketing campaigns focused primarily on engaging, retaining, and growing value from existing customers since 1 January 2024.

G57. **Engaged Community of the Year**<br>Recognizes brands for creating content, experiences, platforms, news, etc. that get their communities to grow, engage, share, act or amplify messaging in a way that directly relates to a brand's goals.

G58. **Influencer Marketing Campaign of the Year**<br>Recognizes campaigns targeting influencers who have an exponential effect on a brand’s audience.

G59. **Marketing Disruptor of the Year**<br>For campaigns that grew their business/brand by changing the marketing model in ways that drive the industry forward.

G60. **New Product or Service Introduction of the Year**<br>For campaigns conducted to introduce a new product or service to the marketplace.

G61. **Omni-Channel Marketing Strategy of the Year**<br>Highlights campaigns seamlessly integrating multiple platforms like digital, print, and in-person.

G62. **Purpose-Led Marketing Campaign of the Year (New for 2026)**<br>Recognizes initiatives built around long-term commitments to social causes, advocacy, or corporate responsibility aligned with core brand values.

G63. **Re-Branding/Brand Renovation of the Year**<br>For campaigns that recreated or repositioned an existing brand.

G64. **Small-Budget Marketing Campaign of the Year**<br>Budget less than €3 m/USD $3 million.

G65. **Viral Marketing Campaign of the Year**<br>For campaigns that used word-of-mouth, video stunts, and other viral tactics as their primary means of communication.

G66. **Youth Marketing Campaign of the Year**<br>For campaigns targeting the youth market (up to age 24).
