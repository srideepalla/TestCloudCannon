These categories recognize the best podcasts since the beginning of 2024.

Nominations that won in The 2025 International Business Awards® may be resubmitted for consideration in the 2026 awards. If they have been updated with recent achievements, they may be submitted to the same categories in which they won. If they have not been updated, they must be submitted to categories different from those in which they won.

**Information to be submitted online for entries in these categories in the 2026 competition include:**

**1\. The podcast itself - no more than three (3) episodes - in the form of URLs to streaming content or uploads of files, which you must attach to your entry through our attachment/link uploading tool<br>2\. Written answers to the following questions, describing the nominated achievements since 1 January 2024:**

a. The date on which this nominated podcast was first distributed or otherwise made public. *Required*<br>b. Describe the podcast and its output since the beginning of 2024 (up to 1,500 characters). *Required*<br>c. Optionally you may list the creative and production credits for your nominated podcast - the people who help to bring it to life. People such as, among others: Advertising Agency/City, Client, Art Director, Audio Engineer, Cinematographer, Creative Director, Designer, Director, Host(s), Illustrator/City, Music Producer/City, Photographer/City, Printer/City, Producer, Production Company/City, Programmer, Videographer, Writer, Web Development Firm/City (up to 1,500 characters). *Optional*<br>d. Reference any attachments of supporting materials throughout this nomination and how they provide evidence of the claims you have made in this nomination (up to 1,500 characters). *Optional*

**3\. Optional (but highly recommended), add to your entry up to 10 webpage links and/or document uploads to support your case to the judges.These might be press articles, press releases, videos, work samples, internal or external reviews, or employee feedback, for example.**

## Features

Recognizes outstanding elements of podcasts, including Best Host, Best Writing, and Best Branded Podcast or Segment, and more.

Z49. **Best Branded Podcast or Segment**<br>The most engaging, entertaining, and/or informative podcast created on behalf of a brand or organization.

Z50. **Best Co-Hosts**<br>The hosts with the most; who demonstrate the best qualities of hosting a podcast interview or conversation. Podcast must have at least (2) hosts.

Z51. **Best Creator or Influencer Series**<br>The most memorable, entertaining, informative, and engaging podcast series featuring a Creator or Influencer.

Z52. **Best Host**<br>The host with the most; who demonstrates the best qualities of hosting a podcast interview or conversation.

Z53. **Best Indie Podcast**<br>The most memorable, entertaining, informative, and engaging podcast created and produced by independent groups or individuals.

Z54. **Best Indie Podcast - Limited Run**<br>The most memorable, entertaining, informative, and engaging limited-run podcast created and produced by independent groups or individuals (3 episodes maximum, trailers and bonus content excluded).

Z55. **Best Individual Episode**<br>The most memorable, informative, or entertaining single podcast episode.

Z56. **Best Limited Series**<br>The most memorable, engrossing, and entertaining multi-episode limited series developed for a podcast. Full series will be judged.

Z57. **Best Live Podcast Recording**<br>Individual or special podcast episode, or podcast mini-series, created for and recorded in a live setting with an audience present. Including but not limited to special live episodes of news and interview shows.

Z58. **Best Multilingual or Cross-Cultural Podcast (New for 2026)**<br>The most engaging and impactful podcast series that regularly publishes in more than one language or intentionally serves cross-cultural or cross-border audiences. This category recognizes shows that effectively bridge cultures, regions, or language groups through their editorial approach, host(s), and distribution strategy.

Z59. **Best Partnership or Collaboration**<br>Individual or special podcast episode, series or mini-series that include a partnership or collaboration between one or more brands.

Z60. **Best Series**<br>The most memorable, entertaining, informative, and engaging podcast series as a whole.

Z61. **Best Video Series**<br>The most memorable, entertaining, informative, and engaging video podcast series as a whole.

Z62. **Best Writing**<br>Podcasts that demonstrate excellence in editorial, regardless of the topic covered.

Z63. **Experimental & Innovation**<br>Individual or series that best uses non-traditional ideas and techniques in concept, content, or execution to produce work that drives the medium forward.

Z64. **Original Music Score/Sound Design**<br>Podcasts that set the industry standard for sound design and effects, or that feature the best original body of music throughout episodes.
