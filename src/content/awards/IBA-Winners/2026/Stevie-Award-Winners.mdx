The winners were determined by the average scores of more than <a href="https:/judges/judging-committees" target="_blank" rel="noopener">300 professionals</a> during two months of judging. Learn about the judging and awards process <a href="https:///judges/judging-awards-process" target="_blank" rel="noopener">here</a>.
