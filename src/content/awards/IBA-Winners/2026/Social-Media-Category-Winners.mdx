These are the Stevie® Award-winning nominations in The 23rd Annual International Business Awards®, which recognizes achievement in the workplace worldwide. The winners were determined by the average scores of more than <a href="/judges/judging-committees" target="_blank" rel="noopener">300 professionals</a> during two months of judging. Learn about the judging and awards process <a href="/judges/judging-awards-process" target="_blank" rel="noopener">here</a>.
