These are the Stevie® Award-winning nominations in The 23rd Annual International Business Awards®, which recognizes achievement in the workplace worldwide. The winners were determined by the average scores of more than <a href="https://judges/judging-committees" target="_blank" rel="noopener">300 professionals</a> during two months of judging. Learn about the judging and awards process <a href="https://i/judges/judging-awards-process" target="_blank" rel="noopener">here</a>.

<strong>Cross-Sector Public Innovation Partnership of the Year</strong>

<em><u>GOLD STEVIE® WINNER</u></em>  
MALAYSIAN RUBBER COUNCIL, Kuala Lupur, Malaysia: Malaysian Rubber Council (MRC): Transforming Malaysia’s Rubber Industry Through a Coordinated Commercialisation Framework  
<em><u>SILVER STEVIE® WINNER</u></em>  
ATREVIA, Madrid, Spain: Bre-B: Moving Trust Before Moving Money  
Dubai Land Department, Dubai, United Arab Emirates: Dubai Real Estate Broker Programme: A Zero-Cost Public-Private Partnership Turning Emiratisation Into a Sustainable Profession  
The Development Bureau of the HKSARG and the Construction Industry Council, Hong Kong, Hong Kong: Hong Kong Infrastructure No. 1  
Taichung City Government Bureau Of Economic Development, Taichung, Taiwan: Taichung Shopping Festival – An Ecosystem of Cross-Sector Collaboration

<strong>Cultural or Economic Exchange Initiative of the Year</strong>

<em><u>GOLD STEVIE® WINNER</u></em>  
Lee Andrews Group, Los Angeles, California, United States: Reimagining Public Transit in Los Angeles’ Most Vulnerable Communities
