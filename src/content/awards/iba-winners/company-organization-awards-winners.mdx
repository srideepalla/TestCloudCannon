Gold, Silver, and Bronze Stevie® Award winners in the 22nd annual IBAs are listed by category, arranged in the following groups of categories. Winners were determined through more than two months of judging by the average scores of [more than 250 professionals worldwide](/judges/judging-committees). Winners will be celebrated during [a gala event](https://stevieawards.com/iba/2025-awards-banquet) in Lisbon, Portugal on Friday, 10 October.

<Image source="/src/assets/images/pcsafclogo-100x.webp" alt="People's Choice Stevie Awards for Favorite Companies Logo" displayWidth="50"/>

Every organization that is honored with a Gold, Silver or Bronze Stevie Award in the Company of the Year categories (categories B01 - B35) will automatically be included in voting for the [**People's Choice Stevie Awards for Favorite Companies**](https://peopleschoice.stevieawards.com/). Voting will be conducted by the general public worldwide from 14 August - 12 September, and the winners will be honored at the IBA awards banquet on 10 October in Lisbon, Portugal. Honorees in the Company of the Year categories will receive instructions on how to promote People's Choice voting to their own customers and stakeholders. *Note that only nominees that receive at least 100 public votes are eligible to win a People's Choice Stevie Award.*
