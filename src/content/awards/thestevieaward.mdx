The Stevie® Award trophy is one of the world's most coveted prizes. Since 2002 the Gold Stevie Award has been conferred for achievement in business to organizations and individuals in more than 60 nations.

In 2012, in celebration of the 10th anniversary of the start of the Stevie Awards movement, we introduced Silver and Bronze Stevie Awards.

In 2013 we introduced the Grand Stevie Award trophy, which will be awarded to only a handful of organizations in each of our competitions.

In each Stevie Awards competition, the top scoring qualifying entry in each category will receive a Gold Stevie Award. Finalists that receive a final average score of at least 8.0 out of a possible 10 will be designated as Silver Stevie winners. All other Finalists will be designated as Bronze Stevie winners.

Gold Stevie winners will automatically receive a Gold Stevie trophy. Silver and Bronze Stevie winners will automatically receive a Silver or Bronze medal, signifying their win, and will have the option to purchase their Stevie trophy. <br />

### **About the Stevie Award Trophy**

The Stevie Award trophy is a symbol of excellence and achievement recognized worldwide. The name Stevie is derived from Stephen, which originates from the Greek word for "crowned," underscoring the award’s celebration of leadership and accomplishment.

The Grand Stevie trophy stands approximately 16 inches tall and is meticulously hand-cast and finished in 24-karat gold. At its core, the trophy features a crystal pyramid held aloft, symbolizing the hierarchy of human needs—a concept developed in the 1960s by psychologist Abraham Maslow. The pyramid represents the progression from basic needs to the pursuit of peer recognition, aligning perfectly with the spirit of the Stevie Awards.
