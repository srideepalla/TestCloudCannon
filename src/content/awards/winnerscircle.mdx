**Winner Logos**

If you use any of these logos to signify one or more Stevie wins in The 2025 International Business Awards® you agree to use them AS IS. You agree to not alter them in _any_ way, other than to resize them. If you wish to render an altered version of one or more of these logos you must first receive [approval from us](mailto:michael@stevieawards.com). Logos may be used for promotional purposes only. They may _not_ be used to produce keepsakes, mementos or mock awards.<br>If you use one or more of these logos online, please link it to open [http://www.StevieAwards.com/IBA](http://www.stevieawards.com/iba) in a new tab or browser window.

**How to Be Profiled in Stevie Awards Media**

Would your Stevie Award-winning organization like to be profiled in the Stevie Awards email newsletter, blog and social media channels? If yes, [**complete this form**](https://stevieawards.com/iba/tell-us-about-your-organization) and our editors will consider your story. There is no cost to be featured if we choose to profile your organization. There is no deadline to complete the form.<br>Our [weekly email newsletter](https://blog.stevieawards.com/stevie-awards-newsletter-grand-stevie-winners-social-media-awards-in-the-stevie-awards-for-women-in-business-stevie-winner-katrina-wurm) has more than 60,000 subscribers, we have tens of thousands of social media followers, and [our blog](http://blog.stevieawards.com/blog) is regularly updated with stories about Stevie winners and judges.<br>[**Complete the form today**](https://stevieawards.com/iba/tell-us-about-your-organization) and our editors will contact you if they wish to profile your organization in our media.

**How to Be Quoted in Stevie Awards Social Media**
