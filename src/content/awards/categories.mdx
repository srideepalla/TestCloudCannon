The International Business Awards® accept entries in scores of categories, grouped as follows.

[**Achievement Categories**](/Awards/Categories/achievement-categories/)<br>Categories to recognize singular business achievements in areas such as business turnaround, finance, revenue generation, and science and technology, among others.

[**Achievement in Management Categories**](/Awards/Categories/achievement-in-management-categories/)<br>Categories recognizing the achievements of entire management teams, groups of managers, and individual executives.

[**Achievement in Marketing Categories**](/Awards/Categories/achievement-in-marketing-categories/)

[**Achievement in Public Relations Categories**](/Awards/Categories/achievement-in-pr-categories/)<br>Categories recognizing achievements since the beginning of 2024 in all corporate communications, investor relations, community affairs, public relations, etc. functions.

[**AI Innovation & Transformation Categories**](/Awards/Categories/ai-innovation-transformation-categories/)<br>**New for 2026.** These categories recognize innovation in the application of artificial intelligence (AI) by individuals and organizations worldwide. Nominations should describe how AI technologies have been applied in innovative ways since 1 January 2024.

[**App Categories**](/Awards/Categories/app-categories/)<br>Categories that recognize excellence in smartphone and tablet apps.

[**Company/Organization Categories**](/Awards/Categories/company-organization-categories/)<br>Categories that recognize the achievements of entire for-profit and non-profit organizations, including Company of the Year, Corporate Social Responsibility Program of the Year, and Fastest-Growing Company of the Year, among others.<br>**There are no entry fees for nominations in the Company of the Year categories (categories B01 - B45).**<br>Stevie Award winners in the Company of the Year categories will automatically be included in public voting for the [People's Choice Stevie Awards for Favorite Companies](/awards/PeoplesChoice/).

[**Creative Categories**](/Awards/Categories/creative-categories/)<br>Categories that recognize the achievements of advertising and marketing agencies, creative departments and teams, and creative leaders.

[**Customer Service Categories**](/Awards/Categories/customer-service-categories/)

[**Entrepreneur Categories**](/Awards/Categories/entrepreneur-categories/)<br>These are categories to recognize individual entrepreneurs, in a variety of industries, and founding teams, in several industry sectors.

[**Event Categories**](/Awards/Categories/event-categories/)<br>Categories that recognize live, virtual, and hybrid events staged since the beginning of 2024.

[**Human Resources Categories**](/Awards/Categories/human-resources-categories/)

[**Individual Categories**](/Awards/Categories/individual-categories/)<br>A variety of categories to recognize the recent achievements of individual professionals

[**New Product & Product Management Categories**](/Awards/Categories//New-Product-Product-Management-Categories/)

[**Podcast Categories**](/awards/categories/podcast-categories/)<br>Categories to recognize the best podcasts in the since the beginning of 2024.

[**Public Sector & Government Innovation Categories**](/Awards/Categories/public-sector-government-innovation-categories)<br>**New for 2026.** These categories recognize innovation by government ministries, public agencies, public enterprises, and municipalities worldwide. Nominations should describe how public sector organizations have designed and implemented innovative approaches since 1 January 2024 that improve public value, citizen experience, or institutional performance.

[**Publication Categories**](/Awards/Categories/publication-categories/)<br>Categories that recognize excellence in annual reports, marketing and sales literature, white papers, and other publications.

[**Social Media Categories**](/Awards/Categories/social-media-categories/)<br>These categories recognize innovation in social media content creation and moderation, management, and marketing.

[**Support Categories**](/Awards/Categories/support-categories/)<br>Categories to recognize the recent achievements of your administrative support, research, maintenance, etc. teams.

[**Sustainability Categories**](/Awards/Categories/sustainability-categories/)<br>Categories to recognize the recent achievements of individuals, teams, and entire organizations to plan, approach, and achievement organizational, industry, or national sustainability goals.

[**Technology Categories**](/Awards/Categories/technology-categories/)

[**Thought Leadership Categories**](/Awards/Categories/thought-leadership-categories/)<br>Categories to recognize organizations that have taken active measures to develop their executives and employees as thought leaders, and promote their thought leadership to audiences inside and outside the organization.

[**Video Categories**](/Awards/Categories/video-categories/)<br>Categories that recognize outstanding video productions since the beginning of 2024.

[**Web Achievement Categories**](/Awards/Categories/web-achievement-categories/)<br>Recognizing excellence in websites and blogs and web-related achievements
