[**Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Technology Categories**](/awards/categories/AI-Technology-Categories) **(New for 2026)**<br>These categories recognize outstanding achievements by women and women-led organizations in the development, implementation, leadership, and impact of artificial intelligence technologies since the beginning of 2024.

[**Company/Organization (Women-Owned or -Led) Awards Categories**](/awards/categories/Company-Organization-Awards-Categories)<br>These categories will recognize the achievements since July 1, 2024 of women-owned and -run organizations worldwide. To be eligible for these categories, an organization must be at least 50% owned by one or more women, **and/or** have a woman as its chief executive and at least 40% of its management team comprised of women.
