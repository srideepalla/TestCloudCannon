#### **It will be emailed to you momentarily.** **<u>You may also access it here</u>**

To make sure you receive important emails from us in the future, when you receive the entry kit email, we recommend that you follow these quick steps:

**Gmail users**—move us to your primary inbox

* **On your phone?** Hit the 3 dots at top right corner, click "Move to" then "Primary"
* **On desktop?** Back out of the email then drag and drop the email into the "Primary" tab near the top left of your screen

**Apple Mail users**—tap on our email address at the top of the email (next to "From:" on mobile) and click “Add to VIPs”

**For everyone else**—<a href="https://help.aweber.com/hc/en-us/articles/204029246-How-can-my-subscribers-safelist-me" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>follow these instructions</strong></a>

### **What's next?**

**Determine Who or What You Want to Nominate, and in Which Categories**

Decide which individuals, departments, teams, achievements, solutions, or implementations in your organization you wish to nominate, then select the most appropriate categories for those entries. For help with category selection, fees, or other questions, contact us at +1 703-547-8389 or [**help@stevieawards.com**](mailto:%20help@stevieawards.com)

When you're ready to enter, create an entry-submission account at <a href="https://the-stevie-awards.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>https://the-stevie-awards.com</strong></a> and follow the instructions to create and save your first entry!
