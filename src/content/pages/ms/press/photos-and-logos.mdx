**Download High-Resolution Images**

Right-click the links below to download high-resolution images of the Stevie® Award trophies and Stevie® Awards for Women in Business logo. These images are suitable for print production, and file sizes are large, so please allow a moment for downloads to complete.

If you use an undated Stevie® Award trophy image in connection with a statement identifying your organization as a Stevie Award winner, please be sure to include the award year, program name, and winning category.

Example: “\[ORGANIZATION NAME\] won a Gold Stevie® Award for Most Innovative Company of the Year - Up to 100 Employees, in The 2026 Stevie® Awards for Women in Business”

**Use of AI-Generated Images**

Stevie® Award winners and judges are welcome to use artificial intelligence (AI) tools to create graphics or other visuals, provided that any images of the Stevie Award trophy, the Stevie Awards logo, or any other Stevie Awards multipurpose winner logos are not AI-generated. All official images of our trophies and logos must be sourced directly from the Stevie Awards to ensure accuracy, quality, and brand integrity.
