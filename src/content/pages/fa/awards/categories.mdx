The Stevie® Awards for Women in Business accept entries in scores of categories, grouped as follows.

[**Achievement Categories**](/Awards/Categories/achievement-categories/)<br>These categories will recognize various types of achievements since the beginning of July 2024. Any organization - not just those that are owned or run by women - may submit nominations to categories J04, J07 and J08. Only organizations that are owned by women (at least 50%) or run by women (leader is a woman or at least 30% of executive team are women) may submit nominations to the other Achievement categories.

[**Achievement in Communications, Marketing or PR**](/awards/categories/achievement-in-communications-marketing-pr-categories/)<br>These categories will recognize the achievements since July 1, 2024 of marketing and public relations programs and campaigns created for or by women.

[**Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Technology Categories**](/awards/categories/AI-Technology-Categories) <span class="red"><em><strong>(New for 2026)</strong></em></span><br>These categories recognize outstanding achievements by women and women-led organizations in the development, implementation, leadership, and impact of artificial intelligence technologies since the beginning of July 2024.

[**Company/Organization (Women-Owned or -Led) Awards Categories**](/awards/categories/Company-Organization-Awards-Categories)<br>These categories will recognize the achievements since July 1, 2024 of women-owned and -run organizations worldwide. To be eligible for these categories, an organization must be at least 50% owned by one or more women, **and/or** have a woman as its chief executive and at least 40% of its management team comprised of women.

[**Individual Women Awards Categories**](/awards/categories/Individual-Women-Awards-Categories/)<br>These categories will recognize the achievements since July 1, 2024 of individual female entrepreneurs, executives and employees.

[**Media (Developed for/by Women) Awards Categories**](/awards/categories/media-awards-categories/)<br>

[**New Product & Service (Developed for/by Women) Awards Categories**](/awards/categories/New-Product-Service-Awards-Categories)<br>These categories will recognize new *and* new-version products and services introduced to the marketplace since July 1, 2024, created by or for women.

[**Social Media Categories**](/awards/categories/Social-Media-Categories/)<br>These categories recognize innovation in social media content creation and moderation, management, and marketing, by and for women, since July 1, 2024.

[**Sustainability Categories**](/awards/categories/Sustainability-Categories)<br>These categories will recognize innovative achievements by individual women and women-led or -owned organizations in resource and environmental sustainability since the beginning of July 2024.
