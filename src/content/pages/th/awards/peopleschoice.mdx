Every organization that is honored with a Gold, Silver or Bronze Stevie Award in the the Company/Organization of the Year categories in the IBAs will automatically be included in voting for the **People's Choice Stevie® Awards for Favorite Companies**. Voting is conducted by the general public worldwide in August - September, and the winners honored at the IBA awards banquet in October. When the results of the IBAs' peer-conducted judging are announced in August, honorees in the Company/Organization of the Year categories are sent instructions on how to promote People's Choice voting to their own customers, employers, followers, and other stakeholders.
