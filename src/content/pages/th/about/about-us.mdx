The Stevie® Awards are the world's premier business awards. They were created in 2002 to honor and generate public recognition of the achievements and positive contributions of organizations and working professionals worldwide. In short order, the Stevie has become one of the business world's most coveted prizes.

There are nine Stevie Awards programs, each with its own focus, list of categories, and schedule. Together they attract more than 12,000 nominations each year from organizations in more than 70 nations and territories.

<a href="https://aba.stevieawards.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The American Business Awards®</a> are open to all organizations and individuals operating in the U.S.A., and feature a wide variety of categories to recognize achievement in every facet of the workplace.

<a href="https://iba.stevieawards.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The International Business Awards®</a> are open to all organizations and individuals worldwide, and include categories to honor accomplishments in all aspects of work life.

<a href="https://asia.stevieawards.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The Asia-Pacific Stevie Awards</a> are open to all organizations and individuals in the 29 markets of the Asia-Pacific region.

<a href="https://gsa.stevieawards.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The German Stevie Awards</a> are open to all organizations and individuals in the European nations in which German is an official language: Austria, Belgium, Germany, Liechtenstein, Luxembourg, and Switzerland.

<a href="https://mena.stevieawards.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The Middle East &amp; North Africa Stevie Awards</a> recognize innovation in the workplace in 18 nations in the MENA region.

<a href="https://hr.stevieawards.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The Stevie Awards for Great Employers</a> are open to all organizations and individuals worldwide, and recognize the world's best companies to work for and the HR teams and professionals, HR achievements, new products and services, and suppliers who help to create and drive great workplaces.

<a href="https://sales.stevieawards.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The Stevie Awards for Sales &amp; Customer Service</a> are open to all organizations and individuals worldwide, and recognize the achievements of sales, customer service, and call center professionals.

<a href="https://tech.stevieawards.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The Stevie Awards for Technology Excellence</a> are open to all organizations and individuals worldwide. The competition recognizes achievements, organizations, and individuals in every facet of the world of technology.

<a href="https://women.stevieawards.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The Stevie Awards for Women in Business</a> are open to all organizations and individuals worldwide, and recognize the achievements of women executives, entrepreneurs, and the organizations they run. In conjunction with this program, we offer the <a href="https://women.stevieawards.com/webinars/upcoming-webinars/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Women|Future Webinars</a> (formerly Women\|Future Conference), a series spread across three months of the year.

## More About the World's Premier Business Awards

Stevie Award judges include many of the world's most respected executives, entrepreneurs, innovators, and business educators. More than 1,000 professionals worldwide participate in the Stevie Award judging process each year. All Stevie Awards entrants have access to all judges' comments about their nominations; an invaluable resource.

There are a [number of benefits](/enter/10-reasons-enter "10 Reasons to Enter") to submitting nominations to Stevie Awards programs. We hope your organization will participate, and look forward to welcoming you into the pantheon of past Stevie Award winners including Acer Inc., Apple, BASF, BT, Ford Motor Company, ING, Procter & Gamble, Roche Group, and Samsung, among many others.

Sponsors of Stevie Awards programs include many leading B2B marketers, publishers, and government institutions.
