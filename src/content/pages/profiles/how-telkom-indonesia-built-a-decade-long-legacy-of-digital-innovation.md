For nearly ten years, Jakarta-based Telkom Indonesia has steadily built one of the most comprehensive digital portfolios in the Asia-Pacific region. Operating at the intersection of public infrastructure and enterprise technology, the organization has collected 246 Stevie Awards across The International Business Awards and The Asia-Pacific Stevie Awards since 2015. That total includes 84 Gold, 74 Silver, and 78 Bronze honors, reflecting an ongoing commitment to modernizing the country's economic and civic backbone.

That modernization spans an extraordinary range of industries, touching everything from rural farming to high-speed sporting events. In the agricultural sector, the Agree Suite earned a Gold award for innovation in business information apps, addressing the digitalization of Indonesia's agricultural economy. A companion initiative, Agree Mart, picked up a Silver in the marketing campaign category for establishing strategic partnerships designed to foster a digital agriculture ecosystem.

Infrastructure and civic projects form another cornerstone of the recognized work. Municipal waterworks gained a digital upgrade through an IoT analytics solution that brought water meter digitization to local utilities, a project that claimed a Gold Stevie in 2023. In the healthcare space, regional telemedicine initiatives accelerated the digital transformation of the country's health sector, earning a Gold honor for excellence in healthcare innovation. Meanwhile, large-scale events received robust technical backing; the organization served as the ICT partner for the Mandalika Street Circuit, an effort recognized with a Gold award for innovations in business-to-business events.

Internal operations and workforce development have also drawn significant peer review. LISA, a one-stop learning ecosystem platform, earned a pair of Gold honors for corporate learning and workforce development alongside its recognition for innovative human resources technology in a public enterprise. On the process automation front, robotic process automation implementations streamlined collection rates, while enterprise resilience efforts were acknowledged when the company strengthened its B2B IT digital services business to drive organizational recovery.

Cultural diplomacy and public data management further round out the portfolio. The Mediahub platform acted as an enabler for Indonesiana TV to promote national cultural diplomacy, taking home a Gold award in the content management solution category. Additionally, the Situation Room for KemenkoPMK leveraged big data solutions to reinforce human and cultural development indexes across the nation.

From expanding broadband and satellite connectivity through solutions like Mangoesky Space to modernizing oil and gas shipping for global energy resilience, the sheer breadth of recognized projects illustrates a sprawling digital footprint. Each category win—from software-defined infrastructure to branded content campaigns—documents a specific technological intervention deployed across the archipelago's commercial and public sectors.
