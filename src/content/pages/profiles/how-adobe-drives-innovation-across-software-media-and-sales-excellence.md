Operating across major hubs including San Jose, San Francisco, New York, Chicago, Pleasanton, and San Mateo, Adobe maintains a strong presence in the computer software and media sectors. The organization's breadth of work spans technical product development, creative applications, sales enablement programming, and public-facing marketing initiatives.

Over ten years spanning from 2017 to 2026, Adobe has accumulated a total of 37 Stevie Awards. This record includes 24 Gold Stevie Awards, 4 Silver Stevie Awards, and 9 Bronze Stevie Awards. The organization's recognized work has been honored across multiple programs, specifically the Stevie Awards for Sales & Customer Service, the Stevie Awards for Technology Excellence, and The American Business Awards.

Recent honors in 2026 highlight the organization's focus on sales development and enablement. Recognition includes a Bronze Stevie Award for Adobe's AI for Sales Enablement Program in the category of Team - AI-Driven Sales Enablement Team of the Year, and a Gold Stevie Award for Adobe's Sales Manager Development Program under Sales Training or Coaching Program of the Year - Technology Industries. Additionally, Adobe received a Silver Stevie Award for Adobe's Agentic AI Enablement Programming in the Sales Enablement Program of the Year category, alongside a Bronze Stevie Award for the corporate event Accelerate 2026: Powering Adobe’s Next Chapter of Sales Excellence in the Corporate & Community - Corporate Event category.

Individual professionals and specific internal teams have also been celebrated in recent years. In 2025, Bruce Ableson, Sr. Director of Global Field Readiness & Enablement, earned a Gold Stevie Award as Sales Training or Education Professional of the Year. The organization's Technical Hands-On Labs received a Gold Stevie Award for Virtual Technology Sales Enablement and Pre-sales Team of the Year, while Adobe's Sales Leadership Lineup earned a Bronze Stevie Award for Best Newsletter. Technical excellence was further recognized with a Gold Stevie Award for Srinivasa Rao Bittla, Senior Software Development Engineer, as Technical Professional of the Year - Computer Software, and a Silver Stevie Award for Arun Pandiyan Perumal, Site Reliability Engineer, in the same category. Pallav Laskar received a Bronze Stevie Award for Firefly Integration: Real-Time AI with Ethical Guardrails under Technology Breakthrough of the Year.

Marketing campaigns and product innovations have secured significant honors in earlier program cycles. The 2024 program year brought widespread recognition for The Adobe 'I Love You, Acrobat' Launch, which earned Gold Stevie Awards in five categories: Marketing Campaign of the Year - Software, Branded Content Campaign of the Year, Influencer Marketing Campaign of the Year, Influencer & Celebrity, and Social Video. Mayuresh Kulkarni was recognized as Product Developer of the Year with a Silver Stevie Award in 2024, and a separate Gold Stevie Award was presented in the category of Lifetime Achievement Award for work titled Revolutionizing Machine Learning and Generative AI in Marketing.

Product and artificial intelligence developments have consistently stood out in earlier evaluations as well. In 2023, Adobe Express for Small Business and Adobe's Frame.io Camera to Cloud (C2C) both received Gold Stevie Awards in their respective categories. Individual contributors Bharatwaja Namatherdhala and Mayuresh Kulkarni were honored as Product Developer of the Year and Technical Professional of the Year, respectively, in 2023. Looking back to 2022, AI and productivity solutions earned top marks, including a Gold Stevie Award for The Power of Using Video Captions - Speech to Text in Adobe Premiere Pro under Best Use of AI & Machine Learning, a Gold Stevie Award for Empowering Creativity with Adobe Creative Cloud Express in Marketing, a Gold Stevie Award for Adobe Fresco in Productivity, and a Silver Stevie Award for Adobe Photoshop Neural Filters in Best Use of AI & Machine Learning.

This extensive record of recognition reflects the diverse scope of Adobe's contributions to software development, artificial intelligence integration, marketing campaigns, and professional sales enablement over the past decade.
