Public records reviewed here cover IBM and its extensive history with the Stevie Awards; no information is available about the exact number of awards won by IBM beyond what is documented in its official records. Headquartered in Armonk, United States, the organization has built an extraordinary multi-decade presence across several core Stevie programs. 

IBM's long-running engagement across international business communities spans prominent initiatives including the Stevie Awards for Sales & Customer Service, The American Business Awards, and The Asia-Pacific Stevie Awards. Across these varied competitions, the organization has accumulated a total of 793 awards, comprising 251 Gold Stevies, 227 Silver Stevies, and 294 Bronze Stevies, spanning from 2003 through 2026.

The breadth of this recognition reflects a wide array of corporate initiatives, technological innovations, and individual leadership excellence. Among the recent 2026 honorees, the organization received significant notice for advancements in artificial intelligence, sales enablement, operations management, and customer service frameworks.

Individual excellence is a recurring theme within the recognized work. In 2026, Ann Sayson earned multiple honors, including a Gold Stevie in the Sales Support Professional of the Year category for the nomination titled "Ann Sayson: Championing Innovation in Sales Support," and an additional Gold Stevie as Most Innovative Operations Manager of the Year for "Ann Sayson: Architect of Operational Excellence." Similarly, Pierce Harriz was recognized with a Gold Stevie in the Young Customer Service Professional of the Year - Technology Industries category.

Technological transformation and the integration of AI form another substantial pillar of the recognized portfolio. Projects such as the "IBM watsonx Workshop" earned Silver Stevies for Best Use of Chatbots or AI Assistants in Sales and Team - AI-Driven Sales Enablement Team of the Year. In customer service and support, initiatives like "IBM TLS transforms the IT support experience with AI and Automation" secured a Gold Stevie for Best Use of AI In Customer Service, while "Customer Case Summarization for Proactive Support" earned a Bronze Stevie for Best Predictive Customer Service Using AI.

Operational excellence and financial solutions also feature prominently in the recognized work. The collaboration detailed in "Transforming Finance - IBM & Verizon" received a Gold Stevie for Business Development Achievement of the Year - Financial Services Industries. Other recognized efforts include the "ELA Attributes Tool," which earned a Gold Stevie for Operations Management Solution, and "The Cognitive Tooling Team: Transforming the IBM Enterprise through AI," which captured a Gold Stevie for Product Development/Management Department or Team of the Year.

The extensive history of recognition across multiple decades demonstrates a sustained organizational focus on enterprise modernization, client engagement, and technical innovation across global markets.
