Cisco Systems operates across a broad spectrum of industries, including computer hardware, software, business services, and telecommunications. With a global presence spanning multiple countries and cities such as Atlanta, Bangalore, Diegem, Dublin, and San Jose, the organization has consistently developed solutions that address complex technological and operational needs.

Over nearly two decades, Cisco Systems has built a substantial record in the Stevie Awards. Since its first winning year in 2005, the organization has accumulated 313 total awards across 19 winning years. This total includes 90 Gold Stevie Awards, 78 Silver Stevie Awards, and 123 Bronze Stevie Awards. Its recognition spans several prominent programs, including the Stevie Awards for Sales & Customer Service, the Stevie Awards for Technology Excellence, the Stevie Awards for Women in Business, The American Business Awards, The Asia-Pacific Stevie Awards, and The International Business Awards.

Recent years highlight the breadth of Cisco Systems' technical achievements and customer service innovations. In 2026, the organization earned honors for projects such as the "Nik Kale, Cisco In-Product AI Engine for Customer Service," which received multiple accolades including a Silver Stevie Award in the category of Achievement - Best Use of Generative AI in Customer Interactions, a Silver Stevie Award for Achievement - Best Use of AI In Customer Service, and a Gold Stevie Award for Achievement in Customer Service Automation. These honors recognize advances in applying generative and in-product artificial intelligence to streamline customer support interactions.

Additional 2026 recognition includes the "Cisco AI Operations (AIOps) for Firewalls" initiative, which secured a Gold Stevie Award for Achievement in the Use of Data & Analytics in Customer Service and a Silver Stevie Award for Award for Innovation in Customer Service - Computer Industries. In live and partner engagement sectors, "Cisco Live 2025 Melbourne" earned a Gold Stevie Award for Award for Innovation in Business-to-Business Events, while the "Cisco Partner Summit 2025: Engineering the AI-Driven Partner Ecosystem" received a Gold Stevie Award in the Corporate & Community - Partner Engagement Event category. Furthermore, the "Fleet-as-Code" nomination was awarded a Gold Stevie Award for Infrastructure as a Service, and the "ADD Agent Kit" collected a Bronze Stevie Award for Software Development Solution.

The organization's 2025 recognitions further illustrate its focus on security, networking, and user experience. Initiatives such as the "Cisco AI Assistant for Support – Intelligent, Omnichannel and In-Product Customer Experience" earned a Silver Stevie Award for Technical Innovation of the Year. In cybersecurity and network infrastructure, the "Cisco Secure Dynamic Attribute Connector" received a Gold Stevie Award for New Service of the Year - Cybersecurity, and the "Secure Adaptive Password Reset (SAPR) Service: Enhancing security and user experience" took home a Gold Stevie Award for Identity & Access Security Solution alongside additional bronze and gold honors.

Individual contributors and technical leads have also been recognized within Cisco Systems' extensive award history. Nominations such as Manevannan Ramasamy as Network Automation & Security Transformation Lead and Nazim Ahmed Khan for Seamless Enterprise Migration to VXLAN-EVPN Networks demonstrate the organization's engagement with technical breakthrough and innovation categories. 

The extensive collection of Gold, Silver, and Bronze Stevie Awards earned by Cisco Systems reflects the organization's ongoing engagement with technical innovation, customer service automation, and enterprise networking solutions across its global operations.
