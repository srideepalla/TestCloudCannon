When an enterprise builds a reputation for global logistics, the underlying engine is rarely just freight and flight paths. Behind every successful delivery sits an intricate apparatus of customer service departments, data analysts, and front-line professionals navigating complex operational demands. Across nearly two decades, stretching from 2007 through 2026, DHL Express Vietnam has maintained an active presence across multiple Stevie Award programs, including the Stevie Awards for Sales & Customer Service, The International Business Awards, and The Middle East & North Africa Stevie Awards.

That extensive history translates into a cumulative total of 834 recognized achievements, featuring 170 Gold, 249 Silver, and 387 Bronze accolades. Rather than reflecting a single successful quarter, this sustained recognition maps an ongoing evolution in how global operations handle everything from routine inquiries to complex logistical hurdles across diverse international markets such as Colombia, Chile, Indonesia, the Philippines, Guatemala, Ecuador, Kuwait, and the United Arab Emirates.

Recent honors highlight the specific ways regional teams adapt to shifting customer expectations. For instance, the 2026 nomination titled *Taking Customer Experience to The Next Level* earned a Gold Stevie in the Achievement in Customer Experience category, while *Customer Service Driving Business Strategy and Growth* picked up a Bronze for Business Development Achievement of the Year in Sustainability. Technological integration also anchors much of the recognized work, seen clearly in the Silver-winning *CX Customs Retention Process Optimization* under the category of Achievement in Customer Service Automation.

Individual contributors frequently anchor the enterprise's broader operational wins. Professional excellence is spotlighted in nominations like *Fauzan*, which claimed a Gold Stevie for Front-Line Customer Service Professional of the Year - All Other Industries, alongside *Aulia Maulina*, who took home Gold for Back-Office Customer Service Professional of the Year - All Other Industries. These individual honors are complemented by department-wide achievements, such as the Gold-winning entry titled *Customer Service - Center Of Excellence* within the Customer Service Department of the Year - Airlines, Distribution & Transportation category.

Behind these specific titles lies a broader organizational structure focused on retention, recovery, and internal culture. Entries like *From Proven Success to Unstoppable Progress* earned Gold for its focus on complaints teams, while the *Employer Of Choice* nomination captured Gold in the Customer Service Employer of the Year category. Additional initiatives such as the *Callback Re-Engament Program* and the data-driven *Service Excellence Fueled by Data analytics team* illustrate the granular approach taken to modernizing contact centers.

The scope of these entries extends far beyond traditional logistics, encompassing sustainability initiatives like *Towards a Sustainable Tomorrow: DHL Express Switzerland's Environmental Initiatives* and targeted regional operations such as *DHL Express UAE's Contact Center*. Together, these programs capture a sprawling, multinational commitment to operational refinement, employee support, and customer satisfaction.
