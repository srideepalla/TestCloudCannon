From Shanghai to global markets, communications agency MSLGROUP has built an expansive presence across the Stevie Award programs, spanning The International Business Awards, The American Business Awards, and The Asia-Pacific Stevie Awards. Across a multi-year run beginning in 2011 and extending through 2017, the network collected 139 total awards, balancing a distribution of 39 Gold, 39 Silver, and 49 Bronze honors.

That multi-year span highlights an operation capable of moving fluidly between internal corporate culture and large-scale consumer engagement. The agency's work in China showcases this breadth, particularly in campaigns that unite regional talent with multinational brands. When IKEA needed to galvanize its Chinese workforce for Talent Focus Week, the team designed the Find Your Extraordinary campaign, earning a Gold Stevie for the innovative use of technology in human resources alongside a Bronze recognition for social media-focused communications.

Brand building and experiential marketing also anchor the agency's portfolio. Collaborating with Luminous Experiential MSLGROUP in Hong Kong, the network brought the Heineken – Shape Your City initiative to life, securing Gold honors for both Best Brand Experience Event and Best Consumer Event. Similarly, Oakley's Turning Green into Gold campaign in China demonstrated strategic dexterity across multiple categories, picking up a Gold Stevie for innovation in the use of celebrities or public figures, a Silver for consumer products PR, and additional Bronze awards in media relations, fashion marketing, and events.

Corporate communications and sponsorship campaigns further illustrate the agency's range. Work for Procter & Gamble brought home recognition, including a Gold Stevie for the #LikeAGirl Olympics in the events and observances category, as well as a Gold for P&G Oral B Genius in personal care marketing and another Gold for the Like A Girl Emojis campaign. General Motors also turned to the team for internal communications and workforce development solutions, resulting in Silver and Bronze honors for the Mind Your Business initiative.

Leadership and strategic vision within the network received individual notice as well. Executive contributions were acknowledged in 2017 with a Silver Stevie for US CEO Ron Guirguis and a Bronze Stevie for Chief Influence Strategist Erin Lanuti, both recognized in executive and innovator categories.

The agency's 2017 campaign output culminated in a notable distinction at The International Business Awards, where the network was named Most Honored Organization in the IBAs. That standing reflects a body of work bridging consumer storytelling, digital strategy, and employee engagement on a global scale.
