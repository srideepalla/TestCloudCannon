Operating at the intersection of computer software and health products and services, Quality Systems, Inc. has established a strong operational footprint across the United States, maintaining locations in Centennial and Irvine. The organization has built a notable reputation for its technological and administrative capabilities within the healthcare sector, addressing complex industry needs through computer software and specialized health services.

Over a multi-year period spanning from 2009 to 2015, Quality Systems, Inc. accumulated a substantial record of 141 total awards in the Stevie Awards. Competing across major programs including The American Business Awards and The International Business Awards, the organization earned 18 Gold Stevie Awards, 29 Silver Stevie Awards, and 54 Bronze Stevie Awards over 7 winning years.

A significant portion of this recognition highlights the organization's broader corporate initiatives and leadership. In 2015, Quality Systems, Inc. was honored with a Grand Stevie Award, placing #3 in the Top 10 rankings. Nominations such as "Quality Systems, Inc.: The Paperless Path Points to Positive Future" earned widespread acknowledgment across multiple categories, including Silver Stevie Awards for Company of the Year in Computer Software, Computer Services, and Health Products & Services, as well as Bronze Stevie Awards in corresponding categories.

Executive leadership and specific operational departments also received prominent accolades during the 2015 program year. The nomination titled "Quality Systems, Inc. President and CEO Sees Paperless Path to Positive Future" earned multiple top honors, securing Gold Stevie Awards for Executive of the Year in Computer Services and IT Executive of the Year, alongside Silver Stevie recognitions in executive and management team categories. Additional organizational pillars were recognized under nominations like "Quality Systems, Inc.'s HR: Cultivating a Culture Shift," which received Bronze Stevie Awards for Human Resources Department of the Year, and individual recognitions such as Donna Greene as Human Resources Executive of the Year and Desiree Vale as Support Staffer of the Year.

The organization's communications and marketing materials have likewise been recognized for excellence. In 2014, Quality Systems, Inc. earned a Bronze Stevie Award in the Best Annual Report - Print category for the "Quality Systems, Inc. 2013 Annual Report. Connect. Care. Thrive." Additionally, collaboration under the Quality Systems and NextGen Healthcare banner produced successful collateral entries, including a Gold Stevie Award for the online brochure "NextGen RCM Services: Focus on care. Thrive. Keys to success in a Changing Healthcare Landscape."

These extensive accolades underscore the scope of Quality Systems, Inc.'s contributions to healthcare technology, corporate communications, and team leadership. The collection of Gold, Silver, and Bronze Stevie Awards highlights the organization's breadth of achievement across software development, executive direction, and operational support.
