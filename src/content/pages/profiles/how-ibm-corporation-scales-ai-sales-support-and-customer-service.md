Public records reviewed here cover IBM Corporation and its Stevie Award history; no information is available about how the company scaled after getting its first Stevie award beyond what is documented in its extensive award record. Spanning business services, computer hardware, software, electronics, and conglomerates, the organization maintains operations across countries such as Brazil, Canada, Germany, India, Japan, Mexico, Poland, and the United States.

Since its first award year in 2003, IBM Corporation has accumulated a total of 769 Stevie Awards across 16 winning years. This total comprises 240 Gold Stevie Awards, 224 Silver Stevie Awards, and 279 Bronze Stevie Awards. The organization has been recognized across multiple programs, including the Stevie Awards for Great Employers, the Stevie Awards for Sales & Customer Service, the Stevie Awards for Technology Excellence, the Stevie Awards for Women in Business, The American Business Awards, The Asia-Pacific Stevie Awards, and The International Business Awards.

Recent recognition highlights specific initiatives in artificial intelligence, digital transformation, and customer service. For instance, the nomination titled "IBM TLS transforms the IT support experience with AI and Automation" received a Gold Stevie Award in the category of Achievement - Best Use of AI In Customer Service. In the realm of self-service and technical support, "AI-Driven Transformation of IBM’s Legal Translation Process" earned a Silver Stevie Award for Achievement in AI-Powered Self-Service, while "IBM Multi-Vendor Systems (MVS) Remote Technical Support uses AI&Automation" secured a Silver Stevie Award for Customer Service Achievement in Automation.

Sales automation and AI-driven sales enablement also feature prominently in the organization's recognized work. The nomination titled "AskQ2C: Revolutionizing Quote-to-Cash Operations" received a Silver Stevie Award for Best Use of Chatbots or AI Assistants in Sales. Additional recognitions in sales support include a Gold Stevie Award for the "IBM watsonx Workshop" in the category of Team - AI-Driven Sales Enablement Team of the Year, alongside a Bronze Stevie Award for "AI Powered Deal Registration" under Achievement in AI-Driven Sales Automation.

Business development and cross-industry collaborations have additionally been recognized. The nomination "Transforming Finance - IBM & Verizon" earned a Gold Stevie Award for Business Development Achievement of the Year - Financial Services Industries. Similarly, "Redefining IBM’s Partner Ecosystem in Emerging Markets" received a Gold Stevie Award for Business Development Achievement of the Year - Technology Industries, illustrating breadth in commercial growth initiatives.

Individual professionals and teams have also been acknowledged across operations. Individual honors include a Gold Stevie Award for Pierce Harriz as Young Customer Service Professional of the Year in Technology Industries, a Gold Stevie Award for Ann Sayson as Sales Support Professional of the Year, and Bronze Stevie Awards for back-office and training professionals such as Kelly King and Marek Liedel. Team recognitions include the "Global Business Partner Management (Global BPM)" team, which earned a Bronze Stevie Award for Sales Operations Team of the Year.

These recognitions collectively document IBM Corporation's ongoing work across customer service, sales operations, artificial intelligence deployment, and professional development within the technology sector.
