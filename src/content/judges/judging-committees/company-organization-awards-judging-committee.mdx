          A highly sought-after global speaker and vanguard environmental
          thought leader, Dr. Achkar developed the Integrated Waste Opportunity

          dimensions. He also pioneered the Digital Waste to Energy Protocol

          infrastructure with sustainability goals. In tandem with his
          non-profit mobilization, Dr. Achkar serves as General Manager for a
          prominent energy services company, directing high-impact engineering
          ventures that specialize in engine-based power generation and
          renewable energy projects.

          he evaluates cutting-edge project proposals for funding. His elite
          academic foundations are supported by advanced credentials and studies
          from world-class institutions, including Harvard University, the
          University of Leicester, Cranfield University, and CentraleSupélec.


          His profound contributions to global industry have earned him top
          professional distinctions. Dr. Achkar holds prestigious Fellowships

          and the Oxford Institute for Energy Studies (OIES). He has also been
          awarded an Honorary Doctorate in recognition of his lifelong devotion
          to international environmental stewardship.
