          evolution and innovation.


          A trusted advisor to CEOs and senior executives at critical business
          junctures, Margy is also a widely published ghostwriter. She has led
          PR initiatives that range from globally coordinated corporate and
          transaction announcements, to sustained visibility programs promoting
          brands and investment locations.


          Before founding Akrete, Margy led the Great Lakes office for a New
          York City-based PR firm and served in multiple PR agencies and
          in-house corporate marketing roles. She also served as a freelance
          journalist published in outlets including The Wall Street Journal,
          Travel Channel, and multiple trade publications.


          Margy spends much of her time outside Akrete serving on Boards of
          Directors advocating for entrepreneurs from all backgrounds and walks
          of life. Most recently she has served as the Chairman of the Board of
          Directors of SomerCor, a leading Small Business Administration (SBA)
          lender, and as a Board Director for the Illinois Manufacturing
          Excellence Center (IMEC), serving on the Governance and Executive
          Committees of both boards. Margy also holds a leadership position on
          the Urban Land Institute’s global Marketing Communications Forum and
          is a regular guest speaker.


          Margy’s professional affiliations have also included the National
          Association of Women Business Owners (NAWBO), Vistage, the Forbes
          Agency Council, Commercial Real Estate Executive Women (CREW), The
          Goldie Initiative, CoreNet Global, and a variety of other community
          and industry organizations. Margy lives near Chicago, Illinois (USA)
          and enjoys global travel and genealogy research.

          Business School, and a Master’s Degree in Urban Policy from the

          has completed marathons on multiple continents and Ironman triathlons
          worldwide.
