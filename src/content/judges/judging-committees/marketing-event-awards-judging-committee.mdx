          Nidhi Alexander is the Chief Marketing Officer at Hexaware
          Technologies, where she has led marketing and analyst and advisor

          Her professional roots are in journalism. She began her career at Star
          Television (News Corp), contributing to programs including Focus Asia
          and National Geographic Today, before making the transition into
          technology marketing. The discipline of those early years—how to frame
          a story, what an audience needs to hear—has remained a through-line

          marketing career at Infosys, where she worked across field marketing,
          academic relations, and employer branding. She later joined Mindtree
          as a leading analyst and global head of advisor relations before
          taking on broader leadership responsibilities.


          At Hexaware, Nidhi's focus has been on building a marketing function
          with genuine commercial accountability—one measured on pipeline
          contribution and brand credibility, and central to that has been
          analyst and advisor relations. Alongside this, she has deepened
          executive client engagement through ForeSight, Hexaware's annual
          leadership forum, and established the Customer Advisory Board to give
          senior clients a structured role in shaping the company's strategic
          direction. She has also overseen the integration of advanced AI across
          the 85-person team's workflows, freeing the function to operate at a
          more strategic level.


          Under her leadership, Hexaware has achieved approximately 140% brand
          value growth since 2021, became the second-fastest-growing IT services
          brand among Indian peers in 2026, and debuted in—then retained—the
          Brand Finance Top 25 global IT services ranking.


          Her work has earned recognition across multiple international awards
          programs, with the marketing team winning Marketing Department of the
          Year and Nidhi receiving individual recognition for her body of work.


          She holds a bachelor's degree in English Literature (Honors) from the
          University of Delhi and a postgraduate diploma in English Journalism
          from the Indian Institute of Mass Communication. She is a committed
          advocate for diversity, equity, and inclusion, and takes pride in
          Hexaware being recognized as one of the Economic Times’ Best
          Organizations for Women four times since 2023.
