          also through  authentic relationships and experiences that genuinely
          resonate with customers. Through strategic marketing, CX innovation,

          Driven by the goal of helping improve the country’s global
          competitiveness, Jed fosters a culture of collaboration, creativity,

          corporate social responsibility (CSR) initiatives. Recognizing the
          need for accessible mentorship and practical business guidance outside

          businesses, communities, and ordinary citizens to better understand
          emerging technologies and embrace the future of digital innovation.

          multi-year program of Eastern Communications in partnership with
          unconnected.org and the Department of Education (DepED). Focused on
          bridging the digital divide, the program delivers high-speed
          connectivity and digital literacy support to schools in Geographically
          Isolated and Disadvantaged Areas  (GIDA) across Luzon, Visayas, and
          Mindanao. As of 2026, the initiative has successfully connected and
          equipped 10 underserved school sites with fully operational satellite
          internet devices, empowering students and educators through access to
          digital opportunities.
