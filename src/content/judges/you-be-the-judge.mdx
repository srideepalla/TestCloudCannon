We invite you to apply to be a judge for one or more of the nine Stevie® Awards programs. Stevie Awards judges gain unparalleled insights into the achievements of leading organizations and professionals worldwide.<br /><br />If you qualify, you'll be assigned categories based on your experience, function, and industry. As a judge, you'll have the opportunity to:

* **Expand Your Knowledge:** Discover innovative strategies and solutions implemented across various industries.
* **Enhance Your Professional Profile:** Receive a certificate of appreciation and have your photograph and biography showcased on the awards website.
* **Network with Peers:** Connect with a global community of business leaders and experts.

**Eligibility Criteria:**

1\. Proficiency in English.<br />2\. Commitment to follow provided instructions and guidelines. <br />3\. Attendance and acknowledgment of a judging orientation meeting online.<br />4\. Agreement to the Judging Terms & Conditions.<br />5\. Willingness to review and score at least 50 nominations, presented in written or video format.

Judging is conducted online, allowing you to review and rate as many nominations as you like, at your convenience. Judging applicants may be asked to evaluate sample entries during the application process.
