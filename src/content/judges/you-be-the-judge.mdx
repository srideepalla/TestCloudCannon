We invite you to apply to be a judge for the 2026 Stevie® Awards for Sales & Customer Service.<br /><br />If you qualify, you'll be assigned categories based on your experience, function and industry. Judging is conducted online, allowing you to review and rate as many nominations as you like, at your convenience, December 2025 to late January 2026.

**Eligibility Criteria:**

1. Demonstrated history of, and verifiable professional experience in one or more relevant industries
2. Proficiency in English.
3. Commitment to follow provided instructions and guidelines.
4. Attendance and acknowledgment of a judging orientation meeting online.
5. Agreement to the [**Judging Terms & Conditions**](http://stevieawards.com/stevie-awards-judging-terms-conditions?_gl=1*1xtlqhs*_gcl_au*MTA5NDQzMTMzLjE3ODI0NTQ2MjQ.*_ga*NDI0MjM0MDYwLjE3Nzk3OTQ4MjU.*_ga_75EDJ0F52B*czE3ODMxNzU4ODckbzIxJGcxJHQxNzgzMTc4ODA2JGozOCRsMCRoMA..).
6. Willingness to review and score at least 50 nominations, presented in written or video format.

Judging applicants may be asked to evaluate sample entries during the application process. As a judge, you'll learn how other organizations and individuals are achieving success and confronting challenges, you'll receive a certificate of appreciation, your photograph and biography will be featured on the awards website, and you'll be showing your support for international business. Plus, you'll have a chance to win two free tickets to the March 5, 2026 awards dinner in New York, along with $1,000 toward the cost of travel and lodging (Terms & Conditions apply).
