We invite you to apply to be a judge for the 2027 Stevie® Awards for Sales & Customer Service.<br /><br />If you qualify, you'll be assigned categories based on your experience, function and industry. Judging is conducted online, allowing you to review and rate as many nominations as you like, at your convenience, November 2026 to mid March 2027.

**Eligibility Criteria:**

1. Demonstrated history of, and verifiable professional experience in one or more relevant industries
2. Proficiency in English.
3. Commitment to follow provided instructions and guidelines.
4. Attendance and acknowledgment of a judging orientation meeting online.
5. Agreement to the [**Judging Terms & Conditions**](http://stevieawards.com/stevie-awards-judging-terms-conditions).
6. Willingness to review and score at least 50 nominations, presented in written or video format.

Judging applicants may be asked to evaluate sample entries during the application process. As a judge, you'll learn how other organizations and individuals are achieving success and confronting challenges, you'll receive a certificate of appreciation, your photograph and biography will be featured on the awards website, and you'll be showing your support for international business. Plus, you'll have a chance to win two free tickets to the April 20, 2027 awards dinner in New York, along with $1,000 toward the cost of travel and lodging (Terms & Conditions apply).

## **Apply To Judge**

To apply as a judge for the 2027 Stevie® Awards for Sales & Customer Service, you must <a href="https://the-stevie-awards.com/login" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong><u>create an account or log in to your existing account here</u></strong></a>**<u>.</u>** After logging in, ensure that *Stevie® Awards for Sales & Customer Service* is selected as the program, then click **“Apply to Judge”** to open the application.
