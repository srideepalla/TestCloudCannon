The Entry Kit will be emailed to you right away. The kit contains information about how to choose the right categories, tips for preparing successful nominations, and additional helpful resources.

Requesting the Entry Kit will not obligate you to submit nominations.

Note that your personal information will be used only for awards communication per our strict [Privacy Policy](/privacy "Privacy Policy").
