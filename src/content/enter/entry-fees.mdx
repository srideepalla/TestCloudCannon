Entry fees for all categories in the 2026 Stevie® Awards for Women in Business are summarized in a PDF document which you may download here:

**SAWIB26EntryFees.PDF**

A separate fee is required for each category in which an entry is submitted. No refunds will be made after July 30, 2026.

Entries submitted after July 22 will be assessed a late fee of $55 (per entry), in addition to the entry fee, but will not be penalized in any way during judging. The late fee is reflected in the PDF file.

**<u>Acceptable Methods of Payment</u>**

* ***Credit Card***: American Express, MasterCard, and Visa
* ***Cheque***: Written on a U.S. bank in U.S. dollars, payable to Stevie Awards, Inc.
* ***Wire Transfer***: Payment in U.S. dollars may be wired. An invoice with bank-transfer instructions will be available in the Purchase History section of your entry account after submission.
