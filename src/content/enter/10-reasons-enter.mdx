**1\.** Entering the Stevie® Awards for Women in Business is a cost-effective way to thank and honor your valued employees and to build or burnish your brand.

**2\.** The [entry fees](/enter/entry-fees) are nominal compared to the publicity benefits your organization can gain by becoming a Stevie® Award winner.

**3\.** Small organizations have just as much chance to win a Stevie as large companies do. Every year, household-name companies such as BT, Procter & Gamble, and Unilever are joined in the winners' circle by small and medium-size organizations that are still building their brands.

**4\.** There are dozens of [categories](/awards/categories/) covering every aspect of work life, from management and finance, to marketing and public relations, to product development, customer service, and human resources.

**5\.** If you’re an advertising agency, marketing consultancy or media production company, your work will be reviewed and honored by potential *clients*—not just competitors. There are many [categories](/awards/categories/) for marketing campaigns, websites and blogs, AI innovation & transformation, podcasts, social media, publications, video productions, events, and other media.

**6\.** Many of the world's top business executives will review your nominations during the judging process, and you'll have access to all of their comments about your entries—an invaluable resource.

**7\.** We'll promote your win across our website and media channels. We get more than 80,000 visitors to our sites every month, have more than 30,000 weekly email newsletter subscribers, and many thousands of Facebook, LinkedIn, and X followers.

**8\.** When you win a Stevie Award, you’ll have the opportunity to deliver a <a href="https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLxZrTaLFgyEJX3RWWjtUETSwF6sW7yWtG&amp;si=XrjbtG-NQ3Y5msfZ" target="_blank" rel="noopener">30-second acceptance speech</a> before an audience of 400+ executives from around the world during an internationally broadcast awards ceremony.

**9\.** The [Stevie Award](/awards/thestevieaward/ "Stevie Award") is one of the world's most coveted prizes. It was designed by the same company that makes the Oscar, the Emmy, and other major awards.

**10\.** As a Stevie winner, you'll get a range of [free tools](/Awards/2025-WinnersCircle/) that you can use to promote your award.
