Follow these simple steps to ensure that you submit entries that are worthy of receiving an International Stevie® Award:

**1\. Subscribe to the Free Stevie Awards Weekly Email Newsletter**

The weekly email newsletter is the best way to stay informed about entry deadline extensions, new categories, and other announcements from the organizers of the Stevie Awards. [Click here](/about/newsletter/) to learn more.

**2\. Review the Entry Kit**

The Entry Kit contains all of the information and instructions you will need to prepare and submit your entries. [Click here](/enter/request-the-entry-kit/) to request your Entry Kit.

**3\. Determine Who or What You Want to Nominate, and in Which Categories**

Decide which individuals, groups, or media (websites, apps, videos, events, podcasts, marketing or PR campaigns, etc.) within your organization you will nominate/enter, then select the most appropriate categories for those entries. Contact the Stevie Awards office with your questions about the most appropriate categories, entry fees, and related matters. Contact us at [help@stevieawards.com](mailto:help@stevieawards.com).

**4\. Use Past International Stevie-winning Entries as Models**

Past International Stevie-winning entries are good examples of entries that judges scored highly, and we recommend that you emulate their style, tone, and structure. [Click here](/awards/2025-sawib-winners/) to read the list of 2025 Gold Stevie Award winners.

**5\. Prepare Your Entries**

International Stevie-winning entries are:

* Well written. They are terse and succinct. Their tone is modest, even when describing the most impressive accomplishments.
* Focused on a few accomplishments. Describe just a few accomplishments in detail, rather than listing many accomplishments briefly.
* Accompanied by supporting information. Provide links to online news stories, press releases, or other documents that support the claims made in your entries.

International Stevie-winning entries are *not*:

* Just about "extraordinary" accomplishments. When you read through some of the past International Stevie-winning entries, you will see that many of them simply are about companies and people that have performed well. They are not about extraordinary, world-changing or industry-changing accomplishments. Tell your story well and support your claims, and the judges will score your entries highly.
* Grandiose or hyperbolic. Avoid flowery language and overblown claims. The judges tend to deduct points for immodesty.

**6\. Submit Your Entries by the Entry Deadline**

The early-bird entry deadline is June 24, 2026. The primary entry deadline is July 22, 2026. Late entries will be accepted through August 19, 2026, with payment of a $55/entry late fee.

**7\. Participate in the Judging Process**

Apply to be a judge. As a judge, you will be able to see entries submitted by organizations from around the world and develop a better understanding of the types of entries that score highly. (You will not be permitted to judge entries in categories in which you have submitted entries.) [Click here](/judges/you-be-the-judge/) to learn more and complete the application form.

If your application is accepted, you will be assigned categories based on your function, experience, and language preferences. As a judge, you may evaluate as few or as many nominations as you like, at your convenience, online, from July-August 2026.
