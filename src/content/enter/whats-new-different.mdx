***Eligibility Timeframe:***

* Eligibility extends from July 1, 2024 through February 3, 2027. Nominations should cite achievements made during that window.

***Entry Deadlines***

* **November 12, 2026:** Early-bird entry deadline - entry fees discounted by $45
* **January 13, 2027:** Primary entry deadline. Late fees will apply after this deadline, even for categories for which there are no entry fees.
* **February 3, 2027:** Last day late entries will be accepted with payment of $55 late fee per entry.

***Entry Fees***

* Entry fees have been raised by $10 per submission from the 2026 competition.

***People's Choice Stevie® Awards for Favorite Customer Service Updates***

Starting with the 2027 edition of the Stevie Awards for Sales & Customer Service, winners in the Customer Service Department of the Year categories will be determined through a unique blending of the average scores of our professional judges and the votes of the general public. Nominees in these categories will be given images and other tools that they may use to encourage their customers, communities, employees, and others to vote for their organizations. (The public will see organizations’ names only, not the content of nominations.)

For every 1,000 votes a nomination receives, we will add a full point to the judges’ average score. For example, if a nomination receives an average score from the judges of 7.87, and the organization receives 850 public votes, we will add .85 to the judges’ average, for a final score of 8.72. Each Customer Service Department of the Year nomination that receives 2,000 or more public votes will be awarded a crystal People’s Choice Stevie Award for Favorite Customer Service, in addition to any other award the nomination may win.
