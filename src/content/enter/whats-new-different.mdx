***Eligibility Timeframe:***

* Eligibility extends from July 1, 2023 through January 14, 2026. Nominations should cite achievements made during that window.

***Entry Deadlines***

* **November 5, 2025:** Early-bird entry deadline - entry fees discounted by $45
* **December 4:** Final entry deadline. Late fees will apply after this deadline, even for categories for which there are no entry fees.
* **January 14, 2026:** Last day late entries will be accepted with payment of $55 late fee per entry. Note that this deadline *will <u>not</u> be extended*.

***Entry Fees***

* Entry fees have been raised by $10 per submission from the 2025 competition.
