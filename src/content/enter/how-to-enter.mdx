Entries for the 21st (2027) edition of the Stevie® Awards for Sales & Customer Service is now accepting nominations. To prepare to submit your entries for the 2027 awards, we recommend that you follow these steps.

**1**. [**Request the Entry Kit**](/enter/request-the-entry-kit/)**.** It will be emailed to you right away. The entry kit contains everything you'll need to know to be able to prepare and submit entries.

**2\.** Follow the Stevie Awards in our social media channels, including <a href="http://www.facebook.com/thestevieawards" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Facebook</a>, <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/company/the-stevie-awards" target="_blank" rel="noopener">LinkedIn</a>, [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/thestevieawards) and <a href="http://www.twitter.com/TheStevieAwards" target="_blank" rel="noopener">X</a>, to stay up-to-date with the latest news about the Stevie Awards for Technology Excellence and other Stevie Awards competitions.

**When you have chosen your categories, written your entries according in the Entry Kit and on our website, and are ready to submit them, begin by creating an account in our online entry-submission system. Begin here:**

[**Register**](https://the-stevie-awards.com/)
