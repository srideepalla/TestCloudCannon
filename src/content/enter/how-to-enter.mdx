We are now accepting nominations for the 2026 (23rd annual) edition of the Stevie® Awards for Women in Business. To prepare to submit your nominations for the 2026 Stevie® Awards for Women in Business, we recommend that you follow these steps:

**1**. [**Request the 2026 Entry Kit**](/request-the-sawib-entry-kit/)**.** The Entry Kit will be emailed to you right away. The kit contains everything you need to know to prepare and submit entries. Requesting the entry kit will not obligate you to submit nominations.

**2\.** Follow the Stevie Awards in our social media channels, including <a href="http://www.facebook.com/thestevieawards" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Facebook</a>, <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/groups/2772791/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">LinkedIn</a>, and <a href="http://www.twitter.com/TheStevieAwards" target="_blank" rel="noopener">X</a>, to stay up-to-date with the latest news about the Stevie® Awards for Women in Business and other Stevie Awards competitions.

**When you have chosen your categories, written your entries according to the instructions in the Entry Kit, and are ready to submit them, begin by creating an account in our online entry-submission system.** <a href="https://the-stevie-awards.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Begin here</strong></a>
