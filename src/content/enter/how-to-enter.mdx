Entries for the 21st (2027) edition of the Stevie® Awards for Sales & Customer Service are now being accepted. To prepare to submit your entries for the 2027 awards, we recommend following these steps.

**1**. [**Request the Entry Kit**](/enter/request-the-entry-kit/)**.** It will be emailed to you right away. The entry kit contains everything you need to know to prepare and submit entries.

**2\.** Follow the Stevie Awards on our social media channels, including <a href="http://www.facebook.com/thestevieawards" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Facebook</a>, <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/company/the-stevie-awards" target="_blank" rel="noopener">LinkedIn</a>, [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/thestevieawards) and <a href="http://www.twitter.com/TheStevieAwards" target="_blank" rel="noopener">X</a>, to stay up-to-date with the latest news about the Stevie Awards for Sales & Customer Service and other Stevie Awards competitions.

**Once you have selected your categories and prepared your entries according to the instructions in the Entry Kit and on our website, create an account in our online entry-submission system. Begin here:**

[**Register**](https://the-stevie-awards.com/)
