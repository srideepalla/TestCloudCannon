Entries for the 21st (2027) edition of the Stevie® Awards for Sales & Customer Service will open this summer. To prepare to submit entries for the 2027 awards, you should first <a href="https://sales.stevieawards.com/request-the-sales-entry-kit/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>request an entry kit</strong></a>. The kit will be emailed to you when the competition opens. The kit will contain complete and detailed instructions on how to prepare and submit entries.

There are more than 150 categories to choose from. Organizations and individuals may submit any number of entries to any of the categories.

**Entries must be submitted online, in English.** Once you have written your entries, it will take you a few minutes only to [**create an entry submission account**](https://the-stevie-awards.com/)in our online system and submit your entries. You'll even be able to pay your entry fees online.
