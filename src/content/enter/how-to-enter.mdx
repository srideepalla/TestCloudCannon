Entries for The 2027 (24th Annual) International Business Awards® will be accepted starting next February.

To prepare to submit your nominations for the 2027 IBAs, we recommend that you follow these steps:

**1**. [**Request the 2026 Entry Kit**](/enter/get-entry-kit/)**.** The Entry Kit will be emailed to you in February. The kit contains everything you need to know to prepare and submit entries. Requesting the entry kit will not obligate you to submit nominations.

**2\.** Follow the Stevie Awards in our social media channels, including <a href="http://www.facebook.com/thestevieawards" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>Facebook</strong></a>, <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/groups/2772791/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>LinkedIn</strong></a>, and <a href="http://www.twitter.com/TheStevieAwards" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>X</strong></a>, to stay up-to-date with the latest news about the IBAs and other Stevie Awards competitions.

**When you have chosen your categories, written your entries according to the instructions in the Entry Kit, and are ready to submit them, begin by creating an account in our online entry-submission system.** [**Begin here**](https://the-stevie-awards.com/)
