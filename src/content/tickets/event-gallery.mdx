Winning a Stevie Award gives you and your team the chance to showcase your achievements on a global stage. Winners in the 2026 (20<sup>th</sup> annual) Stevie Awards for Sales & Customer Service were recognized during a gala awards banquet at New York's Marriott Marquis Hotel on March 5, 2026. More than 300 representatives of Stevie-winning organizations, jury members, and sponsors attended the ceremony. While attendance was not required to win, all winners had the opportunity to deliver an acceptance speech on stage and get professionally photographed and interviewed on our red carpet.
